Join the campaign

You do not need permission to start.

Madhesh Next has no membership fee, no party affiliation and no central approval. If you live in Madhesh — or care about it — there is a way in.

Four ways to take part

01

Start a district or palika chapter

Gather five non-partisan people in your municipality. Pick one module. Meet monthly. We will send you the manual.

02

Contribute data

Business registration numbers, budget documents, price lists, employer needs — anything that makes a local economy legible.

03

Write or report

Local journalists, students and researchers documenting enterprise and jobs in any of the 8 districts.

04

Ask one question

The smallest useful contribution: put one economic question to your mayor or ward chair this month, publicly.

Get in touch

Tell us where you are and what you want to work on. A convener will get back to you.

This form is a demo. Connect a form endpoint (Formspree, Netlify Forms, Google Forms) before publishing.

The only rule

Chapters are non-partisan. Members may belong to any party or none, but a chapter does not campaign for candidates, and does not use the Madhesh Next name for party work.
