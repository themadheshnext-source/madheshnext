How the campaign is organised

Four modules

Madhesh Next is deliberately modular. A chapter in Rautahat and a chapter in Saptari run the same four modules, so work is comparable, repeatable and easy for a newcomer to join at any point.

Module 01

Project

Time-bound interventions with a defined outcome

A Project has a start, an end and something you can point at when it is over. It is how a chapter turns an idea into evidence.

Municipal Business Registration Audit

Time how long it actually takes to register a business in your municipality, publish the number, and compare across the district.

One Product, One Palika

Identify and document one product each local level could produce competitively, with costs, buyers and constraints.

Campus–Industry Mapping

Map what local employers need against what the nearest campus teaches, and publish the gap.

Border Market Study

Document what the nearest Indian market buys, at what price, and what Madhesh could supply.

Module 02

Program

Continuing streams of work that run across years

A Program does not end. It is the standing infrastructure of the campaign — the things that must happen every year in every district for the conversation to hold.

District Chapters

A non-partisan citizen group in each of the 8 districts, meeting monthly, open to anyone.

Young Entrepreneurs Circle

Peer support, mentors and finance navigation for people actually trying to start something.

Ask Your Mayor

A standing civic practice: one economic question, asked publicly, answered publicly, every quarter.

Economic Journalism Fellowship

Support for local journalists to report on enterprise, jobs and constraints — not only politics.

Module 03

Poll

The listening module

Poll is how the campaign avoids becoming another set of opinions from the capital. It asks, records and publishes what people in Madhesh actually say about their own economy.

Youth Work & Migration Survey

Why people leave, what would make them stay, district by district.

Small Business Constraints Poll

What actually stops a shop, workshop or farm from growing — asked of the owners.

Town Halls

Open public meetings in each municipality where economic questions are put to those in office.

Citizen Question Bank

A public, growing list of the economic questions citizens want answered.

Module 04

Publication

The knowledge module

Publication is what survives the campaign. Data, briefs and a manual that anyone — including people who disagree with us — can use.

136 Local Level Profiles

One economic profile per local government: what it produces, employs, taxes and lacks.

Madhesh Economic Review

An annual, plain-language account of how the province's economy actually moved.

The Manual

How to start a chapter, run a town hall and audit your own municipality — free to copy.

Municipal Economic Scorecard

Businesses registered, survived, jobs created, revenue growth — comparable across all 136.

Every module runs in every district

A chapter does not need permission to start. Pick a module, pick your municipality, and begin with one question.
